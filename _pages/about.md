**Zebin Yun** is a Ph.D. student at the [Blavatnik School of Computer Science](https://en-exact-sciences.tau.ac.il/computer), [Tel Aviv University](https://english.tau.ac.il/), advised by [Dr. Eyal Ronen](https://eyalro.net/) and [Dr. Mahmood Sharif](https://mahmoods01.github.io/). His research is on the **security and privacy of machine learning** — including adversarial-example transferability, backdoor attacks on foundation models, and privacy-preserving computation.

Before his Ph.D., he earned his M.Sc. at Tel Aviv University and his B.Eng. in Computer Science at the [Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech)](https://www.sustech.edu.cn/en/).
